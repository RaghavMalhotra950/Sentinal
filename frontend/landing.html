How it works

01

Watch

Point a camera panel at a saved video, a YouTube link, or the built-in demo scene. Each panel is its own camera with its own zones.

File · Link · Demo
02

Detect

YOLO finds people and vehicles in real footage. For a fixed camera, motion mode flags anything that moves, even in cartoons or night video.

YOLO · Motion
03

Draw

Click twice for a danger line or a box zone, or place points for any shape. Draw as many as you need on each camera.

Line · Box · Polygon
04

Alert

The moment something touches a line or enters a zone: a red box on the video, a log entry, a beep, and a screen flash.

Instant
